A legacy that continues
to grow

From a soda fountain in Ahmedabad in 1907 to a diversified business group, Vadilal has grown through generations of enterprise. Our journey continues with a commitment to quality, innovation, and the people we serve.

Enterprise shaped by generations

Vadilal Gandhi began with a simple venture and a hand-operated ice cream machine. His son, Ranchod Lal Gandhi, carried the business forward, opening a retail outlet in 1926. The next generation, Ramchandra and Laxman Gandhi, helped transform the family enterprise through expansion and modernization.

Over the decades, this entrepreneurial spirit has taken Vadilal into new markets and businesses. Across food, dairy, chemicals, industrial gases, forex, hospitality, and emerging ventures, we remain guided by a long- term approach to growth.

The values behind our decisions

Quality

We focus on safe, consistent products and dependable services that meet the expectations of our customers.

Affordability

We work to make quality accessible, offering value without compromising the standards our customers expect.

Innovation

We respond to changing needs through new ideas, product development, and improvements in technology and processes.

Trust

We earn confidence through honest relationships, transparent practices, and a commitment to keeping our promises.

Capabilities that support our promise

Our manufacturing facilities bring together production technology, research and development, and quality checks to support our food businesses.

Ice cream manufacturing

Our Bareilly and Pundhra facilities support ice cream production, with an emphasis on hygiene, consistency, and product development.

Frozen and processed foods

Our Dharampur facility supports the Vadilal Quick Treat range, with capabilities across frozen foods, canned products, and ready-to- eat offerings.

Leading with integrity

Our approach to leadership combines a clear view of the future with the discipline to act responsibly. We encourage initiative, invest in technology, and build relationships based on fairness and mutual respect.

We are committed to lawful and ethical business practices, fair employment, and transparent reporting. These principles guide how we work with our people, customers, suppliers, and business partners.
