Behind
Wallaroo Rise.

Bal & Surge Projects is an Australian property development group with a proven track record in planning, delivering, and managing large-scale residential and mixed-use communities across Victoria and South Australia. Our mission is to create sustainable, high-quality developments that strengthen regional economies, support population growth, and enhance local amenity. We operate with a philosophy of partnership and collaboration, working hand-in-hand with councils, infrastructure agencies, and community stakeholders to deliver projects that align with regional growth strategies and planning frameworks. Our developments are designed not only to provide homes and services but to build vibrant, enduring communities that support local prosperity

Why we chose Wallaroo?

We could have built anywhere on the Yorke Peninsula. We chose Wallaroo because the town already had what most developments spend years trying to manufacture a real community, an established identity, and a coastline that genuinely draws people in.

Wallaroo Rise isn't an attempt to create something from nothing. It's an opportunity to give more people access to something that already exists and to do it in a way that adds to the town rather than simply expanding it.

Track Record

Bal & Surge Projects has delivered residential communities across Victoria and South Australia, with a portfolio exceeding $500 million in end value. From Geelong's growth corridors to regional South Australia, the platform brings the same discipline and long-term thinking to every site.

End to End

We don't just acquire land and hand it to others. TerraSquare operates across the full development lifecycle — from site identification and planning approvals through to infrastructure delivery and community handover. That means one accountable team from start to finish.

In It for the Long Haul

Wallaroo Rise will be delivered across five stages over several years. We're not here to sell lots and disappear. The quality of what we leave behind matters to us — because our reputation is built on finished communities, not launch events.
