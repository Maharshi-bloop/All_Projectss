Oil & Gas

From well pads to processing facilities, Envana brings structure to fragmented emissions data. With OGMP 2.0 alignment, site-specific workflows, and built-in transparency, our platform supports upstream emissions management for upstream, midstream, and downstream operators. It helps meet expectations without overhauling internal systems and functions as part of a broader oil and gas emissions tracking approach.

Industrial Manufacturing

Global manufacturers face pressure to disclose emissions data across plants, fleets, and supplier networks. Envana gives teams a single source of truth, helping align disclosures with CSRD and product carbon footprint expectations across diverse sites. The platform also functions as environmental compliance software, supporting scalable reporting with integrated carbon emissions tracking software features.
