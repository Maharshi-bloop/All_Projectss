From Space to Action How Our Services Work

Capturing the Unseen

Satleo’s microsatellites in Low Earth Orbit capture high-resolution temperature data in real time, detecting subtle variations within 1 degree Kelvin. Operating day and night and unaffected by atmospheric conditions, our system continuously monitors Earth’s environment.

Industry- Specific Datasets

We curate temperature datasets tailored to specific industries, offering both historical and real-time information with seamless API integration. This empowers organizations to gain actionable insights for UHI, Disaster forecasting and assessment, environmental monitoring, infrastructure analysis, and security applications.

AI-Powered Intelligence

Advanced AI and machine learning algorithms process the captured temperature data to identify patterns, predict trends, and detect anomalies. This intelligent analysis transforms raw data into clear, actionable insights for critical decision-making.

Custom Industry Models

Our bespoke models are designed to meet the unique needs of various sectors—from defense and security to energy and agriculture. By delivering domain-specific intelligence, these models help organizations optimize operations, mitigate risks, and enhance efficiency.

Driving Real- World Impact

Our insights don’t just stay on dashboards—they drive action on the ground. Whether it’s issuing early warnings for disaster zones, fine-tuning city planning based on thermal patterns, or enabling farmers to respond to crop stress, Satleo’s intelligence is built for direct, on-the-ground application and decision-making.
