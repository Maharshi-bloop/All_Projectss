Pioneering the Future of Earth Surface Temperature Monitoring and Intelligence

Born from Curiosity. Built for Impact.

Every big change starts with a simple question. For Satleo Labs, it was: How can we understand our planet better to make smarter decisions for tomorrow?

The answer wasn’t immediate, but it was clear. The world was facing challenges; from unpredictable agriculture patterns and rising urban temperatures to increasing environmental risks. Data existed, but it wasn’t enough. It lacked the precision and depth to truly make a difference where it mattered most on the ground, where lives, resources, and futures are shaped.

This realization brought together three minds from diverse backgrounds—Shravan, Dr. Ghosh, and Urmil. Each brought their own experiences, but they shared a common vision: to build a solution that could transform how we understand temperature patterns on Earth. Not just for one industry, but for multiple sectors—from agriculture and defense to disaster management and environmental conservation.

Their belief was simple: what we can see, we can solve. And with the power of space-driven thermal intelligence, they could give industries, communities, and governments the clarity they needed to act—whether it’s predicting crop patterns, preparing for natural disasters, or designing smarter, more sustainable cities.

This idea sparked the birth of Satleo Labs—built on the commitment to turn complex data into clear insights that empower better decisions, safeguard lives, and create a more resilient future.

Our Mission

Our mission is to leverage cutting-edge satellite-based thermal technology and AI-driven analytics to deliver actionable Earth observation insights. We serve urban planners, policymakers, industries, and surveillance agencies, enabling them to make informed, data-driven decisions in climate resilience, agriculture, disaster management, and surveillance. Our commitment to disruptive innovation, ethical data use, and stakeholder-centric solutions sets us apart, ensuring sustainable growth for our employees, investors, and communities. Through relentless technological advancement and strategic partnerships, we strive to shape a smarter, more resilient world

Our Vission

To be a global leader in seamless access to the world's thermal intelligence, transforming the way we perceive and understand our planet. By leveraging this data, we aim to tackle global challenges such as climate change, food security, disaster management, and surveillance while driving sustainability, resilience, and data-driven decision-making for industries, cities, and policymakers.
