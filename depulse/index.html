THE PROVOCATION

What values define the interior designer today?

The profession has accelerated. Materials multiply. Technology moves
faster. Trends arrive and disappear almost overnight. But when everything is possible,
what should guide the choices we make?
THE IDEA

Introducing Mindfulism

Design has always been an act of choice.
What we choose to
make. use. preserve. leave behind.
Mindfulism asks us to bring greater awareness to those choices considering not only how design looks, but what it means, who it serves and what it leaves behind.

FOUR VALUES

Four values. One more aware practice.

01
Integrity Design with honesty, transparency and accountability
02
Curiosity Question conventions. Keep learning. Look beyond the obvious.
03
Craft Value making, material knowledge and the hands behind the work.
04
Responsibility Consider the impact of every choice, on people, resources, culture and the future.
