de.pulse 2.0 The Design Biennale

A platform for ideas, practices and conversations shaping the future of design.

Why

de.pulse 2.0

de.pulse began as a platform for the design community in Ahmedabad, bringing together people, practices, materials and ideas. de.pulse 2.0 builds on that foundation to create a wider, more considered conversation around design and the choices that shape it.

Eight dimensions of awareness.

01
People Design begins with people, their needs, experiences and dignity.
02
Planet Every act of making has an environmental consequence.
03
Materials Understand where materials come from, how they are made and what happens to them next.
04
Craft Preserve knowledge, celebrate making and value the hands behind the work.
05
Health Create environments that support physical, mental and emotional wellbeing.
06
Culture Design carries stories, traditions and identities forward.
07
Resources Use what we have with greater thought and restraint.
08
Future Design with an awareness of what our choices leave behind.
