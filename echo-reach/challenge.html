A legacy industry in need of change.

Future of Stimulation

For over three decades, hydraulic stimulation technology has stood still.

Conventional frac spreads depend on high horsepower, heavy fleets, and millions of gallons of fresh water mixed with polymers and chemical additives, much of which is left behind underground.

Production spikes, then crashes. Fractures close, flow drops, and wells underperform, not because they’re empty, but because conventional tools can’t reach further.

As conventional wells decline and ESG pressures mount, the industry has found itself at an inflection point. Traditional methods are hitting their limits. But where the conventional stops, EcoReach goes further.

We keep saying less is more… it’s so small, and that’s what makes it produce so much more.
