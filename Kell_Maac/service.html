Expert Solutions for All Your Inland Freight Needs Across North America

At Kell-Maac, we specialize in providing a comprehensive suite of logistics solutions that address the dynamic needs of businesses across North America. Whether you're transporting goods within your city or across borders, our services are designed to deliver reliability, efficiency, and cost-effectiveness. From drayage and over-the-road (OTR) freight to break bulk shipping and greenhouse logistics, we tailor our services to ensure your cargo is delivered safely, on time, and within budget.

Comprehensive Logistics Solutions

Drayage Services

In the world of logistics, drayage services play a crucial role in the seamless movement of goods. We specialize in providing reliable drayage solutions that bridge the gap between ports, rail yards, and distribution centers, ensuring your cargo reaches its destination quickly and efficiently.

  • Port Drayage:Swiftly transporting containers from port terminals to local warehouses or distribution centers.
  • Rail Drayage:Efficiently moving containers from rail yards to their final destinations, ensuring timely delivery.
  • Comprehensive Tracking: Real-time tracking and updates on shipment status provide full visibility.
  • Interport Drayage: Facilitating the transfer of containers between different ports, optimizing your shipping logistics.
