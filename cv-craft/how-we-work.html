How We Work

The 4-Step Process

Here’s how we do it
  • 01

    Discovery

    A focused conversation to understand your background, goals, and challenges. We surface strengths, gaps, and priorities.
  • 02

    Crafting

    We build your resume, cover letter, or interview plan tailored to your industry and the North American market. Precision language. Clear structure. Strong positioning.
  • 03

    Review and Refine

    You review the drafts and share feedback. We polish until the documents and messaging reflect your strengths with clarity and authority.
  • 04

    Delivery and Guidance

    You receive the final resume, cover letter, or coaching plan along with practical guidance on how to use it with confidence across applications, networking, and interviews.
