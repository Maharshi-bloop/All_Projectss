A new standard for remote drilling execution.

DRAAS Command delivers turnkey Remote Technical Operations Centers (RTOCs) that centralize experts and AI into execution pods. Unlike traditional remote monitoring, our pods directly control multiple rigs in real time, reducing costs, improving safety, and accelerating performance from planning through production.
