Our Quality Management System (QMS)

At DRAAS Command, quality is not an afterthought, it’s engineered into every process.

Our QMS is built on API Q2 standards, the oil and gas industry’s most rigorous framework for quality, performance, and accountability. Unlike ISO-9001, which is broad and generic, API Q2 goes back to API Q1 (1985) and is designed specifically for upstream oil and gas operations.
