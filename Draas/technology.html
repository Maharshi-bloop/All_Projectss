How It Works

Centrifuge Optimization quantifies barite recovery, base oil losses and recovery, and provides daily totalizers for every fluid and solids stream. These insights give operators a continuous, verifiable record of performance. transforming fluid management from guesswork into actionable metrics.

Vs

Traditional Monitoring

Reactive, manual, and inconsistent across rigs.
  • Manual sampling and delayed reporting
  • Slow response to downhole changes
  • High onsite personnel requirements
  • Fragmented, inconsistent data

DFS Monitoring

Proactive, real-time, and optimized with AI.
  • Continuous flow + density diagnostics with near real-time XRF analysis
  • KPI: Centrifuge Optimization — combines live flow/density with XRF chemistry for true material balance
  • Tracks barite recovery efficiency to minimize waste and improve reuse
  • Monitors base oil losses and recovery for cost and environmental gains
  • Provides daily totalizers for all fluid and solids streams across the system
  • Low-cost, low-maintenance footprint with minimal onsite hardware
DFS delivers hard numbers on efficiency, waste, and cost savings. By optimizing dilution, solids control, and waste disposal while cutting NPT, it sets a new benchmark in next-gen digital fluids management.
