who is alpha energy ?

Company Overview + Mission

view Technologies

Recognized for its agility, technical expertise, and client-centric approach, Alpha Energy Technologies LLC has built its reputation on rapid innovation and performance-based solutions in drilling, completions, and enhanced oil recovery (EOR). Our priority is to optimize the full lifecycle of assets by dramatically enhancing downhole and in-field performance.

Our team comprises seasoned entrepreneurs and industry professionals with decades of experience. Using modern advanced practices to design and manufacture our products, we have been able to push the boundaries of oilfield chemical efficiency.

Our Labs And R&D capabilities

Where is Alpha Energy?

Our operational footprint revolves at our The AET Innovation Center Headquarters in The Woodlands, TX.

Our 11-acre manufacturing facility in Odessa, TX, with a production capacity of over 2.5 million gallons per month, allows us to rapidly manufacture and distribute our technology in the Permian Basin.
