Experimental Conditions

Viscosity measurements measured at 2 gpt in municipal tap water using a Fann-35 style viscometer with an R1B1 bob at 300 sec⁻¹.

Determined through degradative shear sweep analysis on a 40’ flow loop with a 5 gallon reservoir using water containing 5% CaCl₂ to represent general hardness and 95% NaCl to represent electrostatic contributions dissolved to the total ppm range at either 0.5 or 0.75 gpt, as appropriate for the product and water.

*Experimental Products: tested >250k ppm Results may vary with complex produced waters
