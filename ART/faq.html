What is a Flushby?

The Flushby is an innovative automated tool designed to streamline pipe handling operations on drilling rigs. It enables hands-free mechanized flushing of connections, improving operational safety and efficiency while reducing manual labor requirements. This technology represents a significant advancement in automated rig operations.
