ARTIQ Automation Platform

ARTIQ™

Control at the edge. Insight everywhere. A new hardware foundation for modern completions: hands‑free pipe handling, real‑time data acquisition, and standardized control that optimizes execution, reduces exposure, and drives measurable productivity in high‑consequence operations.

  • Deterministic local control that enforces repeatable execution

    Replace manual sequencing with enforced workflows, sequencing, and interlocks—reducing variability and improving operational consistency.

  • Data acquisition & visualization built in (not bolted on)

    ARTIQ™ connects local control and equipment condition monitoring directly to data acquisition and data visualization —so every job generates usable, structured insight.

  • ART‑GN (Global Network) connectivity for real‑time + historical oversight

    Send field data offsite for real‑time monitoring, historical trending, remote diagnostics, and performance review—without sacrificing local control (your system keeps running at the edge).

  • Designed for closed‑loop optimization (AI‑ready architecture)

    Enable offsite analysis and optimization, with insights continuously applied to field operations—advancing from visibility → recommendations → automated setpoint tuning at the wellsite.
