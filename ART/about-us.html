WHO WE ARE

Automated Rig Technologies is a team of industry experts focused on advancing field operations through engineered automation—reducing hazardous exposure while improving consistency, safety, and performance across drilling, completions, and production.

MISSION

Committed to reducing red-zone risk through the
design and deployment of automated tubular handling
systems that enhance safety and operational
efficiency in land-based oil and gas operations.

VISION

To lead the global transition to fully automated,
safer, and smarter energy operations, setting new
standards for safety, efficiency, and execution.
