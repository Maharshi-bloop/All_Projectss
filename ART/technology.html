ARTIQ CONTROL
PLATFORM

ARTIQ is Automated Rig Technologies' unified automation and control platform. It integrates safety-critical control systems, enforced sequencing, and real-time operational intelligence to reduce human intervention and deliver consistent, repeatable performance across drilling, completions, and production operations.

ARTIQ serves as the control and automation backbone for ART systems including JPI, PTI, CD Tong, and integrated production solutions. ARTIQ is designed to control systems, not just monitor them, enforcing safe, repeatable execution in real-world operations.
