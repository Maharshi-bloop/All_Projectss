About Reset Footprint

Universities are more than educational institutions — they shape the minds that will shape the future of our planet.

By teaching the next generation, universities carry the responsibility of leading by example. That’s where Reset Footprint comes in. We believe that institutions guiding the future should be just as committed to protecting it. Our one-stop solution helps universities recruit internationally while minimizing their environmental impact, showing students that sustainability isn’t just a concept—it’s a practice.

With our innovative Footprint Per Lead (FPL) system, universities can now measure and actively reduce the carbon footprint of their recruitment efforts. From eco-friendly printing to sustainable logistics, every aspect of our service is designed to reflect the responsibility universities have to the world. When institutions lead with sustainability, they not only influence their students but also contribute to a greener, more responsible global future.

UnivAssist: A Legacy of Sustainable Recruiting

Since 2010, UnivAssist has led global student recruitment, supporting higher education institutions and high school counselors worldwide. Our mission has always been to enable universities to engage meaningfully with students globally through in-person and virtual programs. Reset Footprint carries forward this legacy, introducing sustainable recruitment practices that align with our vision: to guide and support universities in their international initiatives while minimizing their environmental impact. UnivAssist’s values drive everything we do: constant innovation, learning, acting with integrity, and delivering more than what’s expected. Reset Footprint embodies these values by offering sustainable solutions that go beyond traditional methods of recruitment, ensuring universities leave a positive environmental footprint.
