Your Paper Doesn’t Have to Cost the Planet

Current Scenario 1

Every year, millions of trees are cut down for paper, fueling deforestation, destroying wildlife habitats, and contributing to harmful carbon emissions. Universities across the globe rely heavily on paper, but at what cost to the environment?

Reset Scenario

We don’t just stop at recycled paper. We go further. Along with 100% recycled paper, we partner with suppliers who carry the PEFC/Controlled Forestry certification, ensuring that your paper comes from responsibly managed forests. What’s more, any waste produced during production isn’t simply discarded—it’s sent back to suppliers for recycling, closing the loop and ensuring nothing goes to waste.

Real Impact: Case Studies of Institutions Leading by Example

At Reset Footprint, we believe actions speak louder than words. Here’s how institutions are making a tangible difference through our sustainable solutions:

How UCL Redefines Sustainability with Eco-Friendly Coffee Husk Merchandise

University College London (UCL) prioritized sustainability in its merchandise strategy, partnering with Reset Footprint to create eco-friendly mugs made from upcycled coffee husk. These durable, chemical-free mugs exemplify UCL’s commitment to responsible consumption, carbon reduction, and the circular economy, showcasing how institutions can align practical needs with sustainability goals in impactful ways.

How IC3 Pioneers Sustainability with Eco-Friendly Booklets

The IC3 Conference & Expo, a prestigious annual education event, partnered with Reset Footprint to make its conference booklet more sustainable. By using recycled paper and vegetable-based inks, they reduced environmental impact and made the booklet fully recyclable. This shift highlights IC3's commitment to sustainability, setting a benchmark for eco-friendly practices in education conferences.
