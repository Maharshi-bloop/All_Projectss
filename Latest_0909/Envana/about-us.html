Halliburton-Born. Independently Driven. Carbon Management Reimagined.

Born from Partnership, Built for Progress

Envana was founded as a joint venture between Halliburton, a global leader in energy services, and Siguler Guff, a private equity firm with deep expertise in industrial innovation. Together, they created Envana to deliver a new standard in emissions management by combining technical depth with flexible, forward-looking solutions that help organizations achieve their sustainability goals.

A Team Built for What Comes Next

We’re a purpose-driven team committed to building the tools organizations need to lead with clarity. From sustainability consultants to software engineers, our culture is built on transparency, curiosity, and measurable results.

Our Mission

We equip global industries with actionable insights, carbon management strategies, and audit-ready emissions intelligence so industrial companies can trade manual reporting for strategic advantage and accelerate on optimizing the impact of their operations.

Our Vission

Clarity and strategic action that drive a lower carbon world.
