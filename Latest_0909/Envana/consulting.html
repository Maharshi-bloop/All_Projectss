Born from Partnership, Built for Progress

Envana was founded as a joint venture between Halliburton, a global leader in energy services, and Siguler Guff, a private equity firm with deep expertise in industrial innovation. Together, they created Envana to deliver a new standard in emissions management by combining technical depth with flexible, forward-looking solutions that help organizations achieve their sustainability goals.
