We built the policy tool you’ve probably dreamed up yourself, and never had the time to create.

Our story began in jobs like yours, where the pace was relentless and the tools never kept up.

We know the drill.

Every state’s Session brings a flood of bills, amendments, calendars, and client questions, with no room for error. Whether you're managing 20 clients or supporting 67 caucus members, the pressure is nonstop.

Years ago, our founders Kasey Clary and Zack Krawiec saw this chaos up close as Capitol Pages. Policy staffers, lobbyists, and researchers drowning in PDFs, spreadsheets, andlate-night committee agendas.

That experience stuck with them.
Since then, our team has had hundreds of conversations about the ‘dream tool’ policy experts wish to have during Session.

Legible is the product of those conversations: a tool that makes legislative information instantly accessible and actionable.

The Old Story

  • You start your morning with good intentions, and by 9 am, it’s already triage.
  • Three new bills dropped overnight. A client is panicking about language in an amendment. Committee calendars just updated, again.
  • You’re scanning PDFs, toggling between spreadsheets, and chasing clarity in a system that was never built for speed.
  • There’s no margin for error, but there’s also no real infrastructure. Just a patchwork of sources, and pressure to stay ahead of it all.
  • The process is broken. Not because you're unprepared, but because it was never built for the volume, speed, or pressure you face today.

The New Way

We’re proud to introduce Legible, your AI-native policy intelligence tool designed to bring clarity, speed, and control to your legislative workflow. Legible surfaces the most relevant bills, amendments, transcripts, and deadlines in real time, so you can focus on what matters and act before anyone else.
Built for multi-client lobbyists, caucus staff, and policy professionals, Legible gives you a single, streamlined workspace that replaces PDFs, spreadsheets, and browser tab chaos. No extra hires. No lag. Just instant insight, so you never miss a thing.
