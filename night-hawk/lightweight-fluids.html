We Take a Different Path

By chemically reducing the base fluid density, our Lightweight Fluid System achieves extremely low target densities without requiring nitrogen. The result is a simpler, safer, and more cost-effective solution for the unique challenges of depleted and low-pressure wells.

Unlike providers who focus only on how “light” they can make the fluid, Nighthawk emphasizes bubble stability and chemistry integrity, ensuring consistent results where others fall short.

Two Applications. One Proven System.

Lightweight fluids designed for both completions and production efficiency.

Lightweight Drillouts

Applied during the completion phase of new wells, lightweight drillouts use the system to efficiently clear plugs and debris, ensuring a clean wellbore ready for production. The result is faster completions, reduced downtime, and minimized fluid losses compared to conventional systems, delivering cost savings and accelerated time-to-production.
