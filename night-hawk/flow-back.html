Nighthawk Flowback Equipment

Nighthawk Completion Services offers a range of flowback equipment designed to manage well pressure, recover fluids, and protect well integrity. From flowback tanks for fluid collection to separators and manifold systems for efficient flow control, our equipment supports smooth and reliable operations at every wellsite.

Durable Flow Control with Precision Gear Operation The TSI Gear-Operated Plug Valves offer smooth, controlled operation, allowing operators to fine-tune flow conditions with precision in demanding flowback scenarios.

For hands-on control, our TSI Manually Actuated Plug Valves provide a dependable solution for adjusting and maintaining flow conditions on-site.

Available in various lengths, our 2” and 3” 1502 Iron is rated up to 15,000 PSI, ensuring rugged durability and compatibility with high-pressure flowback applications.
