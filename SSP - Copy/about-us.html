    Industries Served

    Provide timeline that explores the milestones that shaped Sun’s legacy, from early chemical innovation in the 1970s to today’s advanced nanocomposite and proppant technologies.

    • 1977

      Copolymer bead patent issued for Lubra-Glide drilling fluid lubricant. Lubra-Glide drill beads patented and commercialized.

    • 1986

      Bead recovery technology introduced to improve operational efficiency.

    • 1988

      BlacKnite commercialized as the original liquid gilsonite product for wellbore stability.

    • 1996

      First copolymer reactor dedicated to oilfield production installed in the U.S.

    • 1998

      Acquisition of Coastal Superior Solutions expands product and manufacturing capabilities.

    • 2001

      Manufacturing methodology patent awarded for chain-entangled polymers and rapid-rate polymerization.

    • 2009

      Manufacturing capacity expanded with the addition of Reactors B and C at Belle Chasse, LA.

    • 2010

      Advanced manufacturing methodology patent issued for thermoset polymer processes.

    • 2017

      Resiflex synthetic graphite product line introduced for wellbore stability.

    • 2018

      OmniProp® neutrally buoyant proppant product line introduced.

    • 2022

      Lubra-Slide® product line added; bead recovery unit redesigned; UAE operations established.

    • 2024

      Reactor D added at Belle Chasse facility, increasing plant capacity by ~50%.

    • 2025

      DOE funding; ISO 9001:2015 certification completed.

    • 2026

      Lithium sorbent manufacturing begins;

    HSE & Commitment

    At Sun Specialty Products, HSE is more than a requirement, it’s a commitment woven into every part of our business.
