The Problem

You're not just short on time. You're expected to know everything and be everywhere, all at once.
Thousands of bills.  Endless documentation. Each one with the power to impact your stakeholders. But you're only one person, with limited time and unlimited expectations. To maintain trust, you have to know what’s happening, speak to it confidently, and prove your value on demand. You’re not just missing time. Every missed detail is a missed opportunity to lead.

Our Solution

Legible AI gets you the exact information you need, when you need it, so you can get back to advocating and influencing.
  • Track bills, amendments, and hearings in real time
  • Analyze legislation in plain language, with version comparisons and amendment summaries
  • Prioritize what matters to each client or stakeholder with custom tags, alerts, and dashboards
  • Report with branded exports, shareable portals, and ready-to-send client updates

No more manual grunt work. Just smarter insights, delivered instantly, not eventually.
