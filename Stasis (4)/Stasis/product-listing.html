Helix™ Intelligent MPD Control System

Helix is an in-house engineered system that delivers precise annular pressure control by managing surface backpressure. Scalable and flexible, it supports rig circulation, MPD operations, and broader applications like well control and frac flowback—ensuring safe, efficient performance in any environment.
