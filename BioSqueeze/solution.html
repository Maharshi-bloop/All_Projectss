In various applications, DPAS BioSqueeze® solves

  • Annular
    leaks

    Downhole BioSqueeze® overcomes the challenge of annular leaks by injecting biomineralization fluids into leakage pathways. These fluids deeply penetrate microchannels in the cement and form robust crystalline seals, effectively blocking gas migration and restoring zonal isolation.

  • Leaking
    Plugs

    Downhole BioSqueeze® resolves leaking plugs by infiltrating voids and fractures within the plug structure. The biomineralization process reinforces the plug with durable crystalline materials, sealing leaks and ensuring long-lasting integrity without requiring plug replacement.

  • Mechanical
    Integrity

    Downhole BioSqueeze® addresses mechanical integrity issues by targeting and sealing microscopic pathways that compromise the bond between cement and casing. The process strengthens the existing structure, enhancing its resilience under extreme operational conditions and preventing future failures.
