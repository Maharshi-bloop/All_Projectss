The next big thing

Problem Unwanted inflow can result in reduced hydrocarbon production (poor sweep efficiency), accelerated degradation (scaling and corrosion), and increased costs (separation, treatment, disposal)
Solution BioSqueeze® fluids follow the path of least resistance, permanently reducing permeability through deposition of calcium carbonate (reversible with acid)
Result High permeability zones are sealed, diverting injection into upswept reservoir intervals = More Hydrocarbons = More $$$$$

Problem Unconsolidated sand can result in equipment
damage (wellbore deposition and flowback to
surface), and increased costs (separation,
treatment, and disposal)
Solution BioSqueeze® can be injected into
unconsolidated zones to bind particles in place
while maintaining permeability
Result Sand is bonded in place, reducing flowback
and increasing run-time

Problem Recompletions and diverters are expensive
and produce minimal returns (intercept
existing fractures)
Solution BioSqueeze® fill entirety of existing fractures
with calcium carbonate, allowing the well to be
refractured
Result Sealed fractures are stronger than the
formation, forcing new fractures to take
different paths into undisturbed areas
