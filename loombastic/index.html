STUDENT TESTIMONIALS

Meet your teacher!

Hi, I’m Anjali also known as Miss Loombastic.

I didn’t start as an “artist.” In fact I spent 15 years in the corporate world until the urge to create overtook me. 
Like most of us, I started curious, confused, and unsure if I could even do this.  Video tutorials felt confusing, YouTube videos were incomplete, but from here and there I managed to learn what was needed.. As I kept learning, unlearning and creating, I realised something.  Amigurumi became my calm, my creative space, and eventually something much bigger a journey that connected me with people across the world.

And this course? It’s everything I wish I had when I was starting.

  • No overwhelm. No guessing. No feeling stuck.
  • My 5 years of Amigurumi teaching and crafting experience rolled into one live, comprehensive course.
  • Just clear guidance, clear wins, and the joy of creating something with your own hands.
