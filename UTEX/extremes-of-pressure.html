Well Service Packing

UTEX Well Service Packing (WSP) is engineered to maintain pressure integrity inside fluid ends operating under extreme pressure, abrasive slurry, continuous cycling, and high-temperature pumping conditions.

Patented XLH® and SUPERGOLD® technologies help reduce leakage, extend runtime, and lower maintenance frequency in demanding frac and stimulation applications.
Reciprocating Pump Systems

UTEX engineers sealing solutions for reciprocating pump systems operating in some of the industry's most demanding high-pressure environments.

From JFD packings and braided compression packing to V-ring sets and custom sealing technologies, each solution is designed to enhance reliability, maximize uptime, and support long-term equipment performance.
Valve Systems

UTEX delivers engineered sealing solutions for valves backed by decades of application experience across demanding oil & gas and industrial environments.

From advanced spring energized seals to precision elastomer components, we help improve reliability, performance, and service life in critical applications.
Wellheads & Pressure Control Equipment

UTEX supports surface and subsea wellhead and pressure control applications with proven sealing technologies built for harsh operating conditions.

With extensive experience across BOP equipment, gate valves, choke systems, and other critical pressure control assets, we provide durable elastomer and sealing solutions trusted in demanding oilfield environments.
JFD Sealing System

UTEX JFD Packings feature a legacy patented sealing design engineered for reciprocating plunger pumps and rotary applications.

They deliver reliable high-pressure performance, reduced maintenance, extended service life, and proven durability in demanding operating environments.
Downhole Tools

UTEX provides sealing solutions for high-pressure downhole tools operating in some of the industry’s most demanding environments.

From drilling and completions to intervention and production applications, our engineered elastomer and thermoplastic components are designed to withstand extreme pressure, temperature, and aggressive media exposure.
